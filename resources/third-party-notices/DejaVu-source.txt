EVB's annotation editor loads this font, and the native writer embeds the same
bytes in the PDF. Keep packages/contracts/firstUnsupportedAnnotationCharacter.ts in agreement
with the font when replacing it. Its generated ranges describe the Unicode
cmap; native admission also checks the font directly. Missing glyphs fail before
save instead of depending on the current machine's font fallback.
